# Hachibu — Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-11-1 · Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how the Hachibu mobile app ("the App") processes your personal data. It also serves as the information notice required by Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

> Hachibu is an independently developed **portfolio / demo app**. It is not a commercial service and **does not provide medical advice**. Consult a physician or dietitian for decisions about your health.

## 1. Data controller

Umur Paşa (individual data controller)
Contact: umur.pasa@outlook.com

## 2. What data we process

**Account data:** email address, password (stored only in hashed form by the authentication provider; we cannot see it), your name, language preference.

**Health data (special category data):** age, sex, height, weight, target weight, waist/hip measurements; and, *only if you choose to share them*, chronic conditions, medications and allergies.

**Lifestyle and nutrition data:** activity level, sleep, smoking/alcohol use, meal habits, diet style, disliked foods, "weak moment" preferences; your meal, water and weight logs; emergency button usage logs.

**Technical data:** standard access logs kept by the infrastructure provider while the App talks to its server (e.g. IP address, request time).

The App contains **no** advertising, analytics or tracking tools. Your data is never sold or used for marketing.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Creating your account, signing in, password reset | Performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c) |
| Health data | Providing your profile, weight tracking and progress charts | **Your explicit consent** (KVKK Art. 6/2) |
| Lifestyle/nutrition data, logs | Meal/water/weight tracking, progress screens | Performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c); explicit consent where the data qualifies as health data (Art. 6/2) |
| Technical data | Keeping the service secure and running | Legitimate interest (Art. 5/2-f) |

The App's AI and dietitian features are **disabled** in this version; your data is **not sent** to any AI service or dietitian.

## 4. How data is collected

Electronically, through the information you enter in the App.

## 5. Who receives your data (including transfers abroad)

Your data is stored on servers located in **Frankfurt, Germany**, operated by the App's infrastructure (database and authentication) provider **Supabase, Inc.** Your data is therefore **transferred outside Turkey**. Supabase processes it only to provide the service, as a data processor.

Your data is not shared with anyone else, except where required by law.

## 6. Retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all of your data (profile, health information, all logs) is **permanently deleted** from the database.

## 7. Withdrawing consent

You can **withdraw** your explicit consent to the processing of your health data **at any time**. Because the App cannot work without health data, you can withdraw consent by using **Profile > Delete account** in the App, or by writing to the address below. Account deletion page: https://umurpasa.github.io/hachibu/delete-account.html

## 8. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed and request information about it; learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties it is transferred to; request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data; request erasure or destruction; request that these actions be notified to third parties; object to an outcome against you arising solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Send requests from the email address registered in the App to **umur.pasa@outlook.com**. Requests are answered free of charge within **30 days** at the latest.

## 9. Security

Database-level row-level access rules (Row Level Security) ensure only you can access your data; all communication is encrypted (HTTPS).

## 10. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

## 11. Changes

When this policy changes, its version number changes; if a change affects how health data is processed, the App will ask for your consent again.
